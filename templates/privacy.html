Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Spendly helps you track your personal spending. That means we handle information about your money, and we take that seriously. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what the service needs to work:

  • Account details — your name, email address and a hashed version of your password. We never store your password in plain text.
  • Expense records — the amount, category, date and description of each expense you enter.
  • Session data — a cookie that keeps you signed in while you use the service.

We do not connect to your bank, UPI apps or cards. Every expense in Spendly is one you typed in yourself.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

  • Create and secure your account, and let you sign in.
  • Store, display and summarise the expenses you record.
  • Show category breakdowns, totals and date-range filters.
  • Respond to support requests you send us.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or build profiles of you for anyone else.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a database on the server that runs Spendly. Access is limited to the people who operate the service, and only for maintenance and support.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual expenses at any time. If you ask us to close your account, we will delete your account details and expense records within a reasonable period, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts so that pages look consistent across devices. When your browser requests those fonts, Google may receive your IP address and standard request information. No account or expense data is sent to Google.

Beyond this, we do not share your personal data with third parties, except where required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

5. Your Rights

You can at any time:

  • View and edit the information in your account.
  • Delete any expense you have recorded.
  • Ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to delete your account and all associated data.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, make reasonable efforts to notify you.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, email us at privacy@spendly.app. You can also read our Terms and Conditions.
